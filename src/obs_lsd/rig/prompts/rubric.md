
## Evaluator

Run the video of Danny as the feed to the obs-lsd filter, and observe the result.

Score the output in three categories, each with 30 points.

### 1. The Effect Should Be Seamless (30 points)

The halo should seamlessly blend with the floating head and look as though they
are part of the same whole. Deduct points for an obvious dividing line between
the head and the computer-generated graphics. Award points for elements that
blend the two -- having the colored glow of the halo realistically reflected on
the skin of the head, for instance, or having them both lit from the same
angle.

### 2. The Effect Should Not Be Distracting (30 points)

The halo should be visible, but not distract from the expressions on the face.
Deduct points for jumpiness (the halo changes abruptly, rather then slowly
shifting), or other sudden effects. Add points for slow transitions, or gently
touches that take it from the uncanny valley into something that seems natural (perhaps a 3D element to the halo effects, or softer lighting).


### 3. The Effect Should Be Ornate (30 points)

The halo should be complex -- rather than a simple effect, it should have a
pleasant level of detail to it. Think Byzantine iconography, or medieval
woodcuts. It should feel hand-painted, rather than algorithmically-generated.
Deduct points for well-known special effects tricks, and add points for element
that have pleasant attention to details.
